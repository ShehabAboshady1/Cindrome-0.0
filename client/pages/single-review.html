The Boys (2019)

Season 1 • Episode 8 • "You Found Me"
@Shehab_Aboshady
★★★★★ • August 20, 2026
Spoiler

Absolute cinematic masterpiece. The pacing is phenomenal, and Antony Starr deserves all the awards for his performance as Homelander. I can't wait for the next season!

What makes this show stand out isn't just the gore or the shock value, but the incredibly sharp writing that satirizes our current celebrity worship and corporate greed. The Boys takes the concept of "what if superheroes were real" and applies the most cynical, yet realistic, lens possible.

Every character has layers. Butcher is despicable yet you root for him. Hughie is the moral compass constantly being corrupted. It’s a masterclass in character development.
